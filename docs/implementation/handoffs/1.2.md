## Deliverable

Produced in `oms_hub.knowledge.models`:

- `KnowledgeSource(source_document_id, authority_class)`.
- `SourceRevision(source_document_id, source_revision_id, file_sha256, state)`.
- `EvidenceLocator(kind, value)`.
- `EvidenceUnit` with the specified identity, authority, course/exam/lecture
  scope, locator, text, optional image, content hash, source priority, and
  creation/retirement timestamps.
- `SourceRevisionState` and `EvidenceLocatorKind` with every required value.

All models are frozen, slotted stdlib dataclasses. `EvidenceUnit` requires
`course_id` for `course_material`; `supports_medical_claims` is derived and is
true only for course material or published journal. Defaults are
`image_asset_id=None`, `source_priority=0`, UTC ISO `created_at`, and
`retired_at=None`. No speculative locator, ID, or hash validation was added.
`SourceRevision.revision_id` is a read-only compatibility property that returns
the canonical `source_revision_id`; it is not a second persisted field.

## Schema and field ruling

Controller ruling was followed: no exporter or v1 snapshot change was made.

builder and needed by the Task 1.3 repository consumer. The minimum
KnowledgeSource is logical source identity plus authority class; evidence scope
stays on EvidenceUnit. This is reversible through the unapplied additive
proposal `docs/implementation/contract-proposals/CP-0001-knowledge-domain-models.md`.
It proposes `knowledge-v2.json`, lists producers/consumers, preserves v1
backward compatibility, and requires Sol-0 plus every consuming-owner review
before any exporter or activation change. It does not claim approval or
activation.

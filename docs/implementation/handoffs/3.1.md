## Deliverable

Implemented strict, frozen Pydantic Ask contracts in the owned package:

- `AskMode` with `global`, `lecture`, `exam`, `quiz_pre_submit`, and `quiz_post_submit`.
- `AskPageContext` and `QuizPageContext` with immutable objective IDs and strict extra
  rejection.
- `AskRequest` preserving the supplied frozen `RetrievalScope` and its explicit
  `TruthMode.COURSE_ONLY` value.
- `AskThread` and `AskMessage` for scoped conversation identity.
- `GroundedClaim`, `CitationView`, and `GroundedAnswer` with claim evidence IDs,
  citation evidence IDs, insufficient-evidence state, safe-response reason, and provider /
  retrieval provenance IDs.

`QuizPageContext` rejects all known answer-bearing fields before submission, including
`correct_option_id`, `correct_answer_text`, `rationale`, and `is_correct`; `extra="forbid"`
rejects unknown fields as well. Post-submit context can carry those grading fields. Lists
accepted at the test/wire boundary are normalized to immutable tuples. The existing
provider `RetrievalScope`, `EvidenceRef`, and `AnswerEvent` types were imported and left
unchanged.

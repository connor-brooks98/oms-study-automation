## Deliverable

- Produced `QuestionObjective`, `QuestionGenerationRequest`,
  `QuestionEvidenceLocator`, `QuestionPacketEvidence`,
  `QuestionEvidencePacket`, `QuestionEvidenceError`, and async
  `QuestionEvidencePacketBuilder.build(request)`.
- The builder makes one bounded retrieval for the correct-answer concept and
  one for each objective, then re-resolves every provider `EvidenceRef` against
  canonical `KnowledgeRepository` evidence and source-revision records.
- Every accepted reference must bind exactly to a READY revision, unretired
  evidence, requested scope, allowed truth-mode authority, canonical locator,
  byte-for-byte canonical excerpt, and the accepted direct provider checksum
  form `sha256:<EvidenceUnit.content_sha256>`.
- Generation is refused for no concept evidence, no evidence for any requested
  objective, provider-declared insufficiency, generated-artifact authority,
  disallowed literature in `course_only`, unknown evidence/revisions,
  stale/retired revisions, retired evidence, scope or canonical-integrity
  mismatch, more than four objectives in an integrated item, a forbidden target
  signature, or a bound that cannot retain required evidence.
- Equivalent claims deduplicate by an operator-safe Unicode/case/punctuation/
  whitespace-normalized SHA-256 signature while preserving every retained
  evidence ID, revision, authority, checksum, and locator. `<`, `>`, `+`, and
  `-` remain semantically distinct; Unicode comparators/minus and `+/-`
  typographic forms normalize to their ASCII equivalents.
- Every provider result is validated before repository reads and is limited to
  16 references. A completed packet also contains at most 16 total canonical
  evidence IDs/locators after claim grouping, at most 18,000 normalized
  evidence characters, and at most four objectives in every packet mode. A
  request with more than four objectives is refused before retrieval; this
  applies the manifest integration ceiling to every packet because any item
  spanning five objectives is necessarily integrated regardless of its mode
  label.
- Required-cover selection first groups equivalent claim signatures and derives
  exact representative/member options. Its global DP is keyed by at most 32
  role masks (one concept plus four objective bits) and 17 canonical-ID counts,
  retaining the minimum-character state per key with stable priority/ID
  tie-breaks. Group costs are additive, so no signature-dependent future cost
  or arbitrary frontier pruning remains. Required cover and final output
  independently remain at or below 16 canonical IDs. Character projection uses
  the exact highest-priority text that grouping emits, so adding an equivalent
  locator can replace the representative only when the resulting packet remains
  within 18,000 characters. Truncation never slices text and records every
  omitted evidence ID.
- Every selected canonical evidence record is re-resolved immediately before
  packet return, failing closed if revision lifecycle, evidence retirement,
  authority, scope, locator, excerpt, or checksum changed during the build.
- The source snapshot hash is deterministic over the selected canonical
  evidence identities, revisions, authorities, checksums, and locators.
  Prior-tested signatures, forbidden-repeat signatures, and style constraints
  are retained unchanged for downstream generation.
